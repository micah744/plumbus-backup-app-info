Plumbus Private Backups

A personal backup utility that stores encrypted recovery archives in the owner's Google Drive.

The utility runs on the owner's computer. Backup payloads are encrypted locally before upload. Google authorization uses the limited drive.file permission for files created or explicitly opened with this app.

This informational site provides no sign-in, downloads, analytics, or account administration.

Privacy policy

For support, contact the application owner using the support address displayed on Google's consent screen.
