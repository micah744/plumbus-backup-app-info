Plumbus Private Backups: privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. This personal utility stores encrypted recovery archives in the application owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The utility requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits creating, reading, updating, and deleting Drive files created by this app or explicitly made available to it. It does not request general access to unrelated Drive files, Gmail, contacts, or calendars.

Use and storage

Drive access is used to upload encrypted archives, read backup metadata, and retrieve archives for recovery verification or restoration. Backup payloads are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. OAuth credentials are stored in protected local configuration and used for scheduled backup access.

Sharing and retention

The utility does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or send it to analytics services. Encrypted archives are stored by Google Drive in the owner's account. Retention is controlled by the owner and the backup settings.

Owner controls

The owner can revoke authorization in Google Account third-party access settings, remove local credentials, and delete backup files from Google Drive. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete archives already stored in Drive.

Limited Use and support

The utility's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Contact the application owner through the support address shown on Google's consent screen.
